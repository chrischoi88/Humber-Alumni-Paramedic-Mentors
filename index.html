About the Program

The Alumni Peer Mentor Network connects current Humber Paramedic students with recent graduates who offer real-world guidance, encouragement, and mentorship outside of the classroom. This is a volunteer, non-academic support role — not a substitute for professional or clinical counselling.

FAQ

Is this the same as therapy or counselling?

No. Mentors offer informal peer support, not clinical or mental health services. Your faculty liaison can point you to Humber's counselling resources if needed.

Is this mandatory?

No, participation is completely optional for both students and mentors.

Who sees my sign-up form responses?

Only the program's faculty liaison.
