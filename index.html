Important Disclaimer

The Humber Paramedic Alumni Peer Mentor Network is a peer-support initiative designed to foster connection, guidance, and resilience among students and recent graduates.

Alumni Mentors are not licensed counsellors, therapists, or mental health professionals. The guidance they provide is based on personal experience within the Paramedic Program and early professional practice.

This mentorship program does not replace professional medical, psychological, or academic support services. Students seeking professional help are encouraged to contact Humber's Student Wellness & Accessibility Centre or other qualified professionals.

If you need immediate help:

  • Humber Student Wellness & Accessibility Centre: 416-675-6622 ext. 5090
  • Good2Talk (24/7 student helpline): 1-866-925-5454
  • 9-8-8 Suicide Crisis Helpline (24/7): Call or text 988
  • In an emergency: Call 911 or go to your nearest emergency department.

About the Program

The Alumni Peer Mentor Network connects current Humber Paramedic students with recent graduates who offer real-world guidance, encouragement, and mentorship outside of the classroom. This is a volunteer, non-academic support role — not a substitute for professional or clinical counselling.

Become a Mentor

Are you a Humber PCP graduate from the past 1–3 years? Share what you've learned with the next cohort. This is a flexible volunteer role — mentors receive a certificate of participation and are welcome to request a reference letter.

Mentor Eligibility

  • Graduates from the Humber PCP Program within the past 1-3 years.
  • In good standing with the program and professional practice
  • Nominated by faculty
  • Demonstrated professionalism, communication skills, and willingness to mentor.

Mentor Roles

  • Provide informal, non-academic guidance and peer support to current students in various avenues
  • Share practical insights on managing the program, clinical placements, stress, and transition into practice.

Mental Health & Crisis Resources

Mentors are peer supporters, not clinicians. If you or someone you know needs more support than a mentor can offer, these resources are free, confidential, and available now.

In an emergency or if someone is in immediate danger, call 911.

9-8-8: Suicide Crisis Helpline

Free, confidential support for anyone in Canada thinking about suicide or worried about someone else. Available 24/7 by call or text, in English and French.

Call or text 988

Good2Talk (Ontario)

Free, confidential helpline for Ontario post-secondary students, staffed by professional counsellors. Available 24/7.

1-866-925-5454 Text GOOD2TALKON to 686868

Humber Student Wellness & Accessibility Centre (SWAC)

Counselling appointments for Humber students, by phone/video or in person.

416-675-5090 (remote booking, Mon–Fri 8:30am–4:30pm) Lakeshore campus: 416-675-6622 x3331

CMHA Toronto Walk-In Support at Humber

Free walk-in and evening mental health support on campus, provided in partnership with the Canadian Mental Health Association Toronto.

North & Lakeshore campuses, Mon–Fri 5–10pm

FAQ

Is this the same as therapy or counselling?

No. Mentors offer informal peer support, not clinical or mental health services. Your faculty liaison can point you to Humber's counselling resources if needed.

Is this mandatory?

No, participation is completely optional for both students and mentors.

Who sees my sign-up form responses?

Only the program's faculty liaison.
